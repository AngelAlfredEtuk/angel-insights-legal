Privacy Policy

Angel Insights WhatsApp Assistant. Effective October 4, 2026.

This policy explains what personal information the Angel Insights WhatsApp Assistant ("the Service") collects when you message a business that uses it, how that information is used, and how you can ask for it to be deleted. The Service is operated by Angel Insights ("we", "us") in Nigeria.

What we collect

When you send a message to a business number connected to the Service, we receive:

  • your WhatsApp phone number and the profile name shown on your WhatsApp account;
  • the content of your message, and the time you sent it;
  • any order details you include in your message, such as items and quantities.

We do not ask for passwords, payment card numbers or government ID numbers, and the Service is not designed to collect them. Please do not send them in your messages.

How we use it

  • to send you an automatic acknowledgment that your message was received;
  • to sort your message as an enquiry, an order, or something that needs a person;
  • to record orders in the business's order sheet so the business owner can follow up;
  • to forward messages the Service cannot handle confidently to the business owner by email.

Automatic replies and sorting are carried out by software, including an AI model. A person at the business reviews orders and unclear messages before anything is confirmed or promised to you.

Who else handles your information

To provide the Service, your message data passes through the following providers, each under its own terms and privacy policy:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform), which delivers messages between you and the business;
  • OpenAI, which processes the text of your message to sort it into a category;
  • Google (Google Sheets and Gmail), which store order records and deliver escalation emails;
  • our hosting provider, which runs the automation software.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep message and order records only as long as needed to handle your enquiry or order, and no longer than 6 months unless the business has a legal reason to keep them longer.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete the information we hold about you at any time. To do so:

  1. Email alfredgodlycr7@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request".
  2. Include the WhatsApp phone number you messaged from, and the business you contacted if you remember it.
  3. We will confirm by email once your records have been removed from the order sheet, email logs and message records we control. This is normally done within 30 days.

Messages stored in your own WhatsApp app, or held by Meta, OpenAI or Google under their own policies, are outside our control, and you would need to ask those providers directly.

Your rights

Under Nigerian data protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you may ask to see, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, and may object to how it is used. Contact us at the address below to do so.

Security

We limit access to message and order records to the people who need them to run the Service, and we use the access controls of the providers listed above. No online service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
